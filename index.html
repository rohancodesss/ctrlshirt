Good to know

How do I pay?

At checkout you'll get a UPI QR code and a button that opens your UPI app with the amount filled in. After paying, send us your order on WhatsApp with the UPI reference number.

When will it arrive?

Everything is printed after you order. Delivery usually takes days.

Returns?

Because each item is printed for you, we can't take returns for size changes. If your item arrives damaged or misprinted, message us within 7 days with a photo and we'll replace it.

Sizes

Classic regular-fit cotton tees in S to XXL. Not sure which size? Message us on WhatsApp before you order.
